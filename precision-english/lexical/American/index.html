American

Core Meaning Overview

American functions as an adjective for the United States or the American continents and as a noun for a person from the United States.

美国的；美洲的；美国人

American.ADJ2 · ADJECTIVE · of the American continents

Relating to North America or South America, especially in geographical or historical classification.

美洲的；与南北美洲有关的
