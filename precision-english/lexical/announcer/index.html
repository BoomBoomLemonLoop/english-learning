announcer

Core Meaning Overview

A person or broadcast professional who makes announcements or introduces programmes and items.

播音员；现场广播员

Semantic Map

NOUN

  • announcer.N1

Lexical Element Subcards

announcer.N1 · NOUN · broadcast presenter or voice

A person whose job is to introduce programmes, performers, or items on radio, television, or at public events.

介绍节目、项目或发布现场信息的人
