amplify

Core Meaning Overview

Amplify means make a physical signal stronger, intensify an effect, or expand and spread information.

放大；增强；扩大传播

amplify.V2 · VERB · increase an effect or problem

To make an effect, feeling, difference, risk, or problem stronger or more serious.

增强某种效果、问题或风险

Grammar Patterns

  • X amplifies Y
    语法结构：X amplifies Y
    该结构限定了此义项可接的补语、介词或语序。

Collocations

  • amplify the effect
    增强效果
  • amplify inequality
    加剧不平等
  • amplify risk
    放大风险
  • amplify differences
    扩大差异

Examples

  • Poor communication amplified the conflict.
    沟通不畅加剧了冲突。
  • Positive feedback can amplify a small disturbance.
    正反馈会放大微小扰动。

Near-Synonym Contrast

  • amplify vs increase
    Amplify emphasizes making an existing signal or effect stronger, often through a mechanism; increase is the general verb for becoming or making greater.
