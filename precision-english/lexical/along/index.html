along

Core Meaning Overview

Along expresses movement or position following a line or route, and participation or progress in phrasal constructions.

沿着；向前；进展；连同

Lexical Element Subcards

along.PREP1 · PREPOSITION · following a line or route

From one point toward another while staying on or beside a road, river, edge, or similar line.

沿着路线、边缘或线状物

Grammar Patterns

  • along + PATH
    表达“沿着路线、边缘或线状物”的结构：along + PATH
    该结构标明这个意义所要求的补语形式、词序或作用范围；使用时不能只按中文意思自由替换介词或不定式形式。

Collocations

  • along the coast
    preposition+noun phrase
    沿着海岸
  • along the road
    preposition+noun phrase
    沿着道路

Examples

  • We walked along the river.
    我们沿着河边走。
