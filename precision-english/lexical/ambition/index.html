ambition

Core Meaning Overview

Ambition covers both achievement-directed drive and a particular strongly desired goal.

抱负；雄心；目标

Semantic Map

NOUN

  • ambition.N1
  • ambition.N2

Lexical Element Subcards

ambition.N1 · NOUN · strong desire to achieve

A strong drive to succeed, gain power or status, or achieve difficult things, considered as a personal quality.

追求成就的强烈进取心
