Concession and Qualification

让步与限定：承认反向事实，同时控制结论的适用范围与强度。

Core Relation

Concession acknowledges a true proposition P that would normally support an expectation of not-Q, then asserts that Q nevertheless holds. Qualification changes a claim so its scope, degree, frequency, conditions, or certainty match the evidence.

Expression Map

  • Although P, Q.
    Although the route is longer, it is safer.
    P is conceded as relevant background; Q remains the main assertion.
    把第一个事实降为让步背景，并建立反预期关系。
  • Q, although P.
    The plan is workable, although it needs more time.
    Q comes first; P is a later reservation.
    先给结论，再补充保留条件；句末 P 较像追加修正。
  • Even though P, Q.
    Even though the evidence is limited, the pattern is striking.
    Marks a stronger violation of the expectation created by P.
    比 although 更强调 P 本应构成强烈阻碍。
  • P, but Q.
    The route is longer, but it is safer.
    Coordinates two assertions and directs attention toward Q.
    这条路线更长，但更安全。
  • Despite/In spite of + noun phrase or -ing, Q.
    Despite the delay, the team completed the test.
    Compresses the conceded proposition into nominal or non-finite background.
    用名词短语压缩背景；不能直接接完整限定从句。
  • Admittedly, P; nevertheless, Q.
    Admittedly, the sample is small; nevertheless, the result deserves attention.
    Explicitly grants a counter-consideration before preserving Q.
    显式承认反方要点后转回主张，适合论证。
  • While/Whereas P, Q.
    While the method is fast, it is not sufficiently accurate.
    Primarily organizes contrast; while can be concessive when an expectation is recoverable.
    主要表示对比；while 在特定语境可带有限让步色彩，不能自动等同 although。
  • Q, but only if/when/for SCOPE.
    The alternative is useful, but only when speed matters more than precision.
    Asserts Q and then restricts its conditions, time, population, or purpose.
    先提出结论，再明确其条件、时间或范围限制。
  • Q may/can/tends to/is likely to R.
    This approach may reduce short-term costs.
    Qualifies certainty, possibility, or regularity inside the proposition.
    用情态或倾向表达降低确定性或普遍性。
  • In general/for most X/to some extent, Q.
    In general, the policy works well for small teams.
    Makes scope or degree explicit.
    明确限定总体、群体或程度。
  • Granted P, Q.
    Granted the estimate is rough, it identifies the correct order of magnitude.
    Accepts P before advancing Q.
    诚然，这个估算较粗略，但仍识别出了正确数量级。
  • Much as P, Q.
    Much as I value speed, accuracy must come first.
    Concedes an attitude or preference while maintaining a conflicting fact.
    尽管我重视速度，准确性仍必须放在首位。

Semantic Differences

  • concession vs contrast
    Contrast presents difference. Concession additionally activates an expectation from P and asserts Q despite it.
    让步与对比
    Contrast presents difference. Concession additionally activates an expectation from P and asserts Q despite it.
  • concession vs qualification
    Concession preserves Q despite a counter-consideration; qualification changes Q by narrowing scope, strength, frequency, degree, or certainty.
    让步与限定
    Concession preserves Q despite a counter-consideration; qualification changes Q by narrowing scope, strength, frequency, degree, or certainty.
  • although vs but
    Although subordinates P; but coordinates P and Q. Do not normally combine them as Although P, but Q.
    although 与 but
    Although subordinates P; but coordinates P and Q. Do not normally combine them as Although P, but Q.
  • although vs even though
    Even though signals a stronger or more surprising expectation conflict.
    although 与 even though
    Even though signals a stronger or more surprising expectation conflict.
  • although vs despite
    Although selects a finite clause; despite selects a noun phrase or -ing form.
    although 与 despite
    Although selects a finite clause; despite selects a noun phrase or -ing form.
  • although vs while/whereas
    Although conventionally marks concession; whereas marks contrast; while is ambiguous among time, contrast, and weaker concession.
    although 与 while/whereas
    Although conventionally marks concession; whereas marks contrast; while is ambiguous among time, contrast, and weaker concession.
  • however vs nevertheless
    Nevertheless foregrounds persistence despite a counter-consideration; however is a broader contrast marker. Neither licenses a comma splice.
    however 与 nevertheless
    Nevertheless foregrounds persistence despite a counter-consideration; however is a broader contrast marker. Neither licenses a comma splice.
  • may vs tends to vs generally
    May marks possibility; tends to marks a non-universal disposition; generally limits frequency or population coverage.
    may、tends to 与 generally
    May marks possibility; tends to marks a non-universal disposition; generally limits frequency or population coverage.
  • if vs only if
    If P, Q presents P as sufficient; Q only if P presents P as necessary.
    if 与 only if
    If P, Q presents P as sufficient; Q only if P presents P as necessary.
  • admittedly vs defeat
    Admittedly grants a point but does not withdraw the position; the following clause states what survives.
    admittedly 并不表示认输
    Admittedly grants a point but does not withdraw the position; the following clause states what survives.

Sentence Architectures

  • Although P, Q.
    fronted concessive clause
    P frames the sentence; final Q receives main focus
    P 作为已承认背景，主句末端 Q 获得主要信息焦点。
    P frames the sentence; final Q receives main focus
    Although the alarm was loud, nobody responded immediately.
    P 作为已承认背景，主句末端 Q 获得主要信息焦点。
    Although the model is simple, it predicts the main trend.
    虽然模型简单，但它预测出了主要趋势。
  • Q, although P.
    final concessive clause
    Q is established first; P is a retrospective reservation
    先确立 Q，再补充限制或反向背景；语篇重心仍多在 Q。
    Q is established first; P is a retrospective reservation
    The plan is viable, although it requires more staff.
    这项计划可行，尽管需要更多人员。
  • P, but Q.
    coordination
    Both propositions retain main-clause status; order favors Q
    P 与 Q 都作为主句呈现，Q 因顺序与 but 通常更突出。
    Both propositions retain main-clause status; order favors Q
    The alarm was loud, but nobody responded immediately.
    P 与 Q 都作为主句呈现，Q 因顺序与 but 通常更突出。
  • Despite + NP/-ing, Q.
    prepositional concession
    Nominalization backgrounds tense and subject
    把 P 压缩为背景名词短语，适合紧凑书面表达。
    Nominalization backgrounds tense and subject
    Despite the loud alarm, nobody responded immediately.
    把 P 压缩为背景名词短语，适合紧凑书面表达。
    Despite receiving an early warning, the team did not evacuate.
    尽管提前收到警报，团队仍未撤离。
  • P. Nevertheless, Q.
    independent-sentence link
    Separate sentences permit independent elaboration
    两个独立句便于分别展开证据；第二句承载被保留的结论。
    Separate sentences permit independent elaboration
    The alarm was loud. Nevertheless, nobody responded immediately.
    两个独立句便于分别展开证据；第二句承载被保留的结论。
  • While acknowledging P, SUBJECT + Q.
    participial acknowledgement
    The main-clause subject must be the understood acknowledger
    适合正式讨论；分词结构的隐含主语必须与主句主语一致。
    The main-clause subject must be the understood acknowledger
    While acknowledging the uncertainty, the committee approved a limited trial.
    适合正式讨论；分词结构的隐含主语必须与主句主语一致。
  • What remains true, although P, is that Q.
    nominal-clause focus
    The nominal clause explicitly selects the surviving proposition
    用名词性从句和系表结构显式突出经让步后仍成立的命题。
    The nominal clause explicitly selects the surviving proposition
    What remains true, although the data are limited, is that the risk cannot be ignored.
    用名词性从句和系表结构显式突出经让步后仍成立的命题。
  • Q, at least/primarily/only + SCOPE.
    scope modifier
    A modifier narrows Q without adding a counter-proposition
    句子架构：Q, at least/primarily/only + SCOPE.
    A modifier narrows Q without adding a counter-proposition
    The alternative is cheaper, at least in the short term.
    保留核心命题，同时明确适用范围。
  • X that/which meets CONDITION can Q.
    relative-clause restriction
    A defining relative clause restricts the set to which Q applies
    句子架构：X that/which meets CONDITION can Q.
    A defining relative clause restricts the set to which Q applies
    Methods that assume stable demand can perform poorly during sudden shocks.
    假定需求稳定的方法在突发冲击期间可能表现不佳。
  • Q only if P. / Provided that P, Q.
    conditional boundary
    States a necessary or enabling boundary
    句子架构：Q only if P. / Provided that P, Q.
    States a necessary or enabling boundary
    The estimate is reliable only if the input data are complete.
    只有输入数据完整，这项估算才可靠。
  • Adjective as SUBJECT may be, Q.
    inverted concession
    Formal inversion foregrounds a conceded property
    句子架构：Adjective as SUBJECT may be, Q.
    Formal inversion foregrounds a conceded property
    Useful as the index may be, it cannot replace direct measurement.
    这个指标虽有用，却不能替代直接测量。

Transformations

  • The route is longer. It is safer.
    Although the route is longer, it is safer.
    Subordinate the fact that creates the defeated expectation.
    把第一个事实降为让步背景，并建立反预期关系。；把第一个事实降为让步背景，并建立反预期关系。
  • Although the sample is small, the result is consistent.
    Despite the small sample, the result is consistent.
    Convert a finite proposition into a noun phrase.
    clause → noun phrase；隐藏命题 the sample is small。；clause → noun phrase；隐藏命题 the sample is small。
  • Although the method is limited, it is useful.
    Admittedly, the method has limitations; nevertheless, it is useful.
    Expand into an explicit grant-and-preserve sequence.
    把单句扩展为承认—保留结论的论证序列。；把单句扩展为承认—保留结论的论证序列。
  • The policy works.
    The policy tends to work for small teams under stable conditions.
    Add frequency, population, and condition qualifications.
    加入频率、对象和条件三类限定，避免过度概括。；加入频率、对象和条件三类限定，避免过度概括。
  • The evidence proves that the treatment works.
    Although the findings are encouraging, the limited sample suggests only that the treatment may work.
    Match epistemic force to evidence and concede the limitation.
    加入让步，并把证据动词与情态强度调整到证据可支持的水平。；加入让步，并把证据动词与情态强度调整到证据可支持的水平。
  • Although demand changed, the model remained accurate.
    The model remained accurate despite the change in demand.
    Nominalize the event while retaining its participant.
    尽管需求变化，模型仍保持准确。；虽然需求发生变化，模型仍保持准确。
  • The method is effective.
    To some extent, the method is effective for routine tasks, but its performance under high load remains uncertain.
    Add degree, domain, and uncertainty boundaries.
    该方法在一定程度上对常规任务有效，但在高负载下的表现仍不确定。；该方法有效。

Scenarios

  • 应用场景：everyday decision
    Although the apartment is small, it gets plenty of light.
    虽然公寓很小，但采光充足。
    The cheaper option is adequate, but only if you use it occasionally.
    较便宜的方案够用，但前提是只偶尔使用。
  • 应用场景：interpersonal discussion
    I accept that you warned me; nevertheless, the instructions were still unclear.
    我承认你提醒过我；尽管如此，说明仍不清楚。
    Your interpretation may be right, although another explanation is possible.
    你的解读可能正确，不过也可能存在另一种解释。
  • 应用场景：professional recommendation
    While acknowledging the budget constraint, we recommend a limited pilot rather than a full rollout.
    在承认预算限制的同时，我们建议开展有限试点，而非全面推行。
    The procedure is effective for routine cases, but its value in emergencies remains uncertain.
    该程序对常规情况有效，但在紧急情况下的价值仍不确定。
  • 应用场景：scientific reasoning
    Although the variables are associated, the data do not demonstrate a causal relationship.
    虽然变量存在关联，但数据并未证明因果关系。
    The model generally predicts the trend, although it underestimates the highest values.
    该模型总体上能预测趋势，尽管它低估了最高值。
  • 应用场景：questioning a supervisor
    To what extent does this conclusion depend on the sampling method?
    这一结论在多大程度上取决于抽样方法？
    Would the claim still hold if the highest values were excluded?
    如果排除最高值，这项主张仍成立吗？
  • 应用场景：self-correction
    Let me qualify that: the change helps in most cases, not in every case.
    让我限定一下：这项改变在多数情况下有帮助，并非所有情况。
    What I mean is not that the method always fails, but that it may fail under high load.
    我的意思不是该方法总会失败，而是它在高负载下可能失败。

Question Formation

  • Does Q still hold although P?
    Does the conclusion still hold although the sample is small?
    Test whether a claim survives a counter-condition.
    检验反向条件出现后结论是否仍成立。
  • To what extent does Q hold?
    To what extent does this alternative solve the original problem?
    Request a degree or scope boundary.
    要求说明程度边界，避免二元化回答。
  • Under what conditions does Q hold?
    Under what conditions is the cheaper method reliable?
    Request enabling and limiting conditions.
    要求给出适用条件。
  • What evidence would justify the stronger claim that Q?
    What evidence would justify the stronger claim that the treatment is effective?
    Test evidence against epistemic strength.
    把注意力转到结论强度与证据匹配。
  • Would it be more precise to say QUALIFIED-Q?
    Would it be more precise to say that the effect occurs mainly at higher temperatures?
    Offer a collaborative refinement.
    提出协作式精确化建议。

Practice

  • Combine with although so the second proposition is the main assertion: The alarm was loud. Nobody reacted immediately.
    用 although 合并两句，使第二个命题成为主断言：警报声很大。没有人立即反应。
  • Choose although, whereas, or despite and justify the logic and grammar: ___ the two designs look similar, they serve different purposes.
    从 although、whereas 或 despite 中选择，并说明逻辑和语法理由。
  • Correct: Although the alternative is cheaper, but it may be less reliable.
    修正从属连词与并列连词重复。
  • Rewrite with despite: Although the sample was small, the pattern appeared consistently.
    把限定从句压缩成名词短语，并指出被隐藏的命题。
  • Make defensible with at least three qualifications: Online learning is effective.
    至少加入三种限定，使“在线学习有效”这一主张可以成立。
  • Write versions with the concessive clause first and last; explain the focus change.
    分别把让步从句置于句首和句末写出两个版本，并解释焦点变化。
  • Revise: Although the variables are correlated, this proves that A causes B.
    修改这项过度断言：虽然变量相关，但这证明 A 导致 B。
  • Replace Does the method work? with one scope and one condition question.
    把“该方法有效吗？”改写为一个范围问题和一个条件问题。
  • Write: general claim → qualification → conceded limitation → refined conclusion.
    按“总体主张→限定→承认局限→精确化结论”写一段论证。
  • Rewrite to state that complete inputs are necessary: The estimate is reliable if the inputs are complete.
    改写句子，明确完整输入是必要条件。
  • Retract an absolute claim and replace it with a qualified one in two spoken sentences.
    用两个口语句子收回绝对化主张，并以限定后的主张替代。

Model Guidance

  • Although the alarm was loud, nobody reacted immediately.
    Loudness creates an expectation of reaction.
    响亮通常应促使人反应，因此形成反预期。
  • Whereas the two designs look similar, they serve different purposes.
    The base relation is contrast; despite cannot select the finite clause.
    这里核心是外观与用途的对照；若语境强调‘外观相似本应意味着用途相似’，although 也可能成立，但需说出该预期。despite 后不能直接接该完整从句。
  • Although the alternative is cheaper, it may be less reliable. / The alternative is cheaper, but it may be less reliable.
    Use subordination or coordination, not both markers.
    选择从属或并列架构之一。
  • Despite the small sample, the pattern appeared consistently.
    The phrase hides the proposition the sample was small.
    small sample 恢复为完整命题是 the sample was small。
  • Online learning can be effective for motivated adult learners, particularly for well-structured tasks, provided that timely feedback is available.
    The answer limits certainty, population, task, and condition.
    如果能及时获得反馈，在线学习对积极主动的成年学习者可能有效，尤其适用于结构清晰的任务。
  • Although the plan is expensive, it is reliable. / The plan is reliable, although it is expensive.
    Clause position changes discourse sequencing.
    前置版本以成本为背景、以可靠性收束；后置版本先确立可靠性，再追加成本保留。
  • Although the variables are correlated, this does not show that A causes B; it suggests only that a relationship may exist.
    Correlation is not causal proof.
    虽然变量相关，但这并不能表明 A 导致 B；它只说明二者可能存在关系。
  • To what extent does the method work across different tasks? Under what conditions does it become unreliable?
    The questions separately request scope and failure conditions.
    第一问查范围与程度，第二问查失效条件。
  • The alternative is generally cheaper. Its advantage is greatest when demand is stable. Admittedly, it reacts slowly to sudden changes. It is therefore a sensible default, although not the best option for emergencies.
    Frequency, condition, and exception make the conclusion defensible.
    结论经过频率、条件和例外限定后更可辩护。
  • The estimate is reliable only if the inputs are complete.
    Only if marks completeness as necessary.
    只有输入内容完整，这项估算才可靠。
  • That was too broad. The method tends to work for routine cases, but its performance under high load remains uncertain.
    The repair withdraws overbreadth and supplies limits.
    刚才的说法过于宽泛。该方法通常适用于常规情况，但在高负载下的表现仍不确定。
