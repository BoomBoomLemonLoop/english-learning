1. 重点句｜Key Sentences

先抓主干，再看修饰成分
01

Although global variation in cancer profiles demands a nuanced approach to cancer control at national and regional levels, primary prevention must be at the forefront, including intensified efforts to reduce tobacco use, prevent infections, lower alcohol consumption and excess body weight, and increase physical activity.

尽管全球癌症谱存在差异，因此国家和地区层面的癌症控制需要采取更具针对性、兼顾差异的方法，但初级预防仍必须处于核心位置，包括进一步加强减少烟草使用、预防感染、降低酒精摄入和超重，以及增加身体活动等措施。

nuanced approachprimary preventionat the forefrontincluding intensified efforts to
结构：主干：Although A demands B, C must be at the forefront. 句末 including ... 是补充说明 primary prevention 具体包含哪些措施。
02

In 2021, it accounted for nearly one in six deaths (17%) globally, and almost one in four deaths (23%) from noncommunicable diseases.

2021 年，癌症约占全球全部死亡的六分之一（17%），并占非传染性疾病死亡的近四分之一（23%）。

account forone in sixnoncommunicable diseases
结构：account for = 占……比例。one in six = 每六个中有一个，不要机械翻译成“六分之一例”。
03

The disease also imposes substantial societal and macroeconomic costs.

癌症还造成了巨大的社会成本和宏观经济成本。

impose substantial costssocietal costsmacroeconomic costs
结构：impose costs = 使……承担成本 / 带来成本，是论文中非常常见的正式表达。
04

In 2022, premature deaths from cancer resulted in an estimated US $566 billion in global productivity losses, comprising losses from both paid work (US $305 billion) and unpaid activities (US $260 billion), including caregiving and domestic labor.

2022 年，癌症导致的过早死亡估计造成了 5660 亿美元的全球生产力损失，其中包括有偿工作损失（3050 亿美元）和无偿活动损失（2600 亿美元），后者包括照护和家务劳动。

premature deathsresult inproductivity lossescomprising losses from
结构：result in = 导致；comprising ... = 现在分词补充说明损失的构成；including ... 继续细化 unpaid activities。
05

Indeed, nearly one half (48%) of all cancer-related deaths are estimated to be potentially avoidable worldwide, through either primary prevention targeting modifiable risk factors (33%) or early detection and improved access to treatment (15%).

事实上，全球近一半（48%）的癌症相关死亡被估计为具有潜在可避免性：其中 33% 可通过针对可改变危险因素的初级预防来避免，另有 15% 可通过早期发现和改善治疗可及性来避免。

be estimated to bepotentially avoidabletargeting modifiable risk factorsaccess to treatment
结构：be estimated to be = 据估计为……；through either A or B = 通过 A 或 B；targeting ... 是现在分词修饰 primary prevention。
06

Yet the proportion of avoidable deaths varies markedly by world region because of differences in risk factor profiles and the availability of effective interventions.

然而，由于危险因素谱以及有效干预措施可获得性的差异，可避免死亡所占比例在不同世界地区之间存在显著差异。

vary markedly byrisk factor profilesavailability ofeffective interventions
结构：vary markedly by ... = 随……而显著不同；because of 后接两个并列名词成分。
07

In sub-Saharan Africa, for example, the proportion estimated to be avoidable exceeds 60%, reflecting the high burden of cancer deaths that are either largely preventable (e.g., cervical cancer) or highly treatable when detected early.

例如，在撒哈拉以南非洲，估计可避免的癌症死亡比例超过 60%，这反映出该地区癌症死亡负担很高，而这些死亡所对应的癌症要么在很大程度上可以预防（如宫颈癌），要么在早期发现时具有较高的可治疗性。

estimated to be avoidablereflectinghigh burdenwhen detected early
结构：reflecting ... 是结果/解释性的分词结构；when detected early = when they are detected early 的省略，语义上修饰 highly treatable。
08

Consistent with previous reports, our objectives are three-fold:

与以往报告一致，我们的研究目标有三个方面：

consistent withobjectives are three-fold
结构：three-fold 在这里不是“三重的”，而是“三方面的 / 分为三项”。consistent with = 与……一致。
09

Cancer type–specific, aggregated counts at global, regional, and national levels were used to identify the most common cancer types for incidence and mortality at each level.

研究使用了按癌症类型划分、并在全球、区域和国家层面汇总的病例数，以识别各层面发病和死亡最常见的癌症类型。

cancer type–specificaggregated countsat ... levelsbe used to identify
结构：Cancer type–specific 与 aggregated 是两个并列的前置形容词性修饰语，共同修饰 counts；were used to identify 是被动语态 + 目的不定式。
10

Throughout, the terms transitioning, emerging, and lower HDI countries/economies refer to nations classified as low or medium HDI, whereas transitioned or higher HDI countries/economies refer to nations classified as high or very high HDI.

全文中，transitioning、emerging 和 lower-HDI countries/economies 这些术语指被归类为低或中等 HDI 的国家；而 transitioned 或 higher-HDI countries/economies 则指被归类为高或极高 HDI 的国家。

throughoutrefer toclassified aswhereas
结构：Throughout = 在全文中；refer to = 指；whereas = 而、相比之下；classified as ... 是过去分词短语修饰 nations。
11

Finally, we project the global cancer burden to 2050 using demographic projections while assuming constant incidence or mortality rates.

最后，我们利用人口学预测将全球癌症负担推算至 2050 年，同时假定发病率或死亡率保持不变。

project ... to 2050using demographic projectionswhile assumingconstant incidence or mortality rates
结构：科研英语高频结构：We + verb + object + using B + while assuming C. using 表示方法，while assuming 表示计算时同时采用的假设条件。
12

Geographic and socioeconomic variation in the burden and profile of cancer was examined across 20 aggregated regions defined by the United Nations Population Division and by countries classified according to the HDI, based on the 2022 Human Development Report from the United Nations Development Program.

研究考察了癌症负担及癌症谱在地理和社会经济层面的差异：一方面按联合国人口司定义的 20 个汇总区域进行分析，另一方面按 HDI 对国家进行分类；该 HDI 分类依据联合国开发计划署《2022 年人类发展报告》。

geographic and socioeconomic variationburden and profile of cancerclassified according tobased on
结构：这是典型科研写作中的名词化 + 被动语态。Geographic and socioeconomic variation ... 是长主语；was examined 是谓语。句末 based on ... 被逗号隔开，作为补充信息，说明 HDI 分类依据。

a nuanced approach to ...
针对……采取细致、因地制宜的处理方法
nuanced 不宜死记“微妙的”；论文中常表示考虑差异和复杂性的。
be at the forefront
处于最重要/最前沿的位置
表示某事应被优先考虑。
account for ...
占……（比例）

impose substantial costs
造成/带来巨大的成本

result in ...
导致……
结果关系，后接名词。

由来自 A 和 B 的损失构成

4. 难句与科研句法｜Sentence Patterns

你这次阅读中最值得掌握的结构
1. Although 从句 + 主句：先让步，再提出核心结论
Although A, B.

例句：Although global variation ... demands a nuanced approach ..., primary prevention must be at the forefront.

Although 引导让步状语从句。作者先承认全球癌症谱存在差异，再提出不受这一差异动摇的主张：初级预防仍应优先。

2. 现在分词短语：including / comprising / reflecting
Main clause, V-ing ...

例句：..., comprising losses from both paid work and unpaid activities.

这些 V-ing 结构通常不承担主句谓语，而是补充说明前面整件事的内容、构成或结果。阅读时先抓主句，再把分词结构作为第二层信息。

3. when detected early：省略的时间状语从句
when + past participle

例句：highly treatable when detected early

可还原为 when they are detected early。这里不是独立的“伴随”，而是说明“在什么条件/时间下高度可治疗”。

4. using ... while assuming ...：方法 + 假设
We estimate/project A using B while assuming C.

例句：We project the global cancer burden to 2050 using demographic projections while assuming constant incidence or mortality rates.

using B 说明采用的工具/数据/方法；while assuming C 说明推算过程中同步成立的假设。两者都附属于主句，但逻辑功能不同。

5. 名词化 + 被动语态：科研方法部分的典型风格
Variation in X was examined ...

例句：Geographic and socioeconomic variation in the burden and profile of cancer was examined ...

自然口语更可能写成 We examined how ... varied ...；论文采用名词化 variation 和被动 was examined，使分析对象成为句子主题。

6. 逗号 + based on ...：补充说明分类依据
..., based on ...

例句：..., based on the 2022 Human Development Report ...

逗号把 based on ... 标记为补充性信息（supplementary information），而不是紧贴某个名词的限定性修饰。对比：the classification based on age 中 based on age 直接限定 classification。

5. 你原笔记中最需要修正的几个点

避免形成错误词义映射
nuanced approach = 微妙的方法
→ 细致、兼顾差异、考虑复杂性的处理方法

nuanced 强调“有层次、考虑细微差别”，不是日常汉语里的“微妙”。

incidence burden = 发病率
→ 发病负担 / 新发病例负担

incidence 单独可指发病率/新发病例；加 burden 后重点转向疾病造成的规模和负担。

three-fold = 三重
→ 有三个方面 / 分为三项

在 “our objectives are three-fold” 中是在数研究目标的维度。

when detected early = 伴随
→ 省略的时间/条件状语从句

可还原为 “when they are detected early”，说明何时具有较高可治疗性。

profile = 简介
→ 谱 / 构成特征 / 分布特征

cancer profile、risk factor profile 都是在讲组成模式，而不是个人“简介”。

based on 前的逗号可忽略
→ 逗号改变修饰关系的读取方式

“, based on …” 更像对前面分析/分类依据的补充，而不是紧贴单个名词的限定修饰。

6. 可直接迁移到科研写作的模板

Methods / Results 常用

A. 方法 + 假设
We estimate A using B while assuming C.
我们使用 B 估计 A，同时假定 C。

你的 CFD/燃烧方向可迁移：
We calculate the flame temperature using equilibrium chemistry while assuming adiabatic conditions.
我们使用平衡化学计算火焰温度，同时假设过程绝热。

B. 名词化方法句
Geographic and socioeconomic variation in X was examined across Y.
研究考察了 X 在地理和社会经济层面的差异。

C. 客观估计
A is estimated to be B. / A was projected to reach B by 2050.
