alloy

Core Meaning Overview

Alloy names a metallic mixture and the process of combining a metal with another element.

合金；把金属制成合金

Semantic Map

Lexical Element Subcards

alloy.N1 · NOUN · metallic mixture

A metal made by combining two or more elements, at least one of which is a metal.

由至少一种金属与其他元素构成的金属材料
