America

Core Meaning Overview

America can denote the United States or, in geographical usage, the American landmass; the plural the Americas removes much ambiguity.

美国；美洲

America.PN2 · PROPER NOUN · the American continents

North America and South America considered together; also used historically or geographically for the lands of the Western Hemisphere.

美洲；北美洲与南美洲整体
