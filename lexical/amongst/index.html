amongst

Core Meaning Overview

Amongst is a regional stylistic variant of among with the same central group-internal relations.

在……之中（among 的变体）

Semantic Map

PREPOSITION

  • amongst.PREP1

Lexical Element Subcards

amongst.PREP1 · PREPOSITION · among; surrounded by or within a group

A variant of among used for location within, membership in, or relations inside a group.

among 的地区性变体；在群体之中
