amuse

Core Meaning Overview

Amuse means cause laughter or pleasant interest, or keep someone pleasantly occupied.

逗乐；使消遣

Semantic Map

使人发笑或感到有趣

amuse.V2 · VERB · keep occupied pleasantly

To keep someone, especially oneself or a child, pleasantly occupied for a period of time.

使某人在一段时间内愉快地消遣

Grammar Patterns

  • amuse oneself by/with + noun/-ing
    语法结构：amuse oneself by/with + noun/-ing
    该结构限定了此义项可接的补语、介词或语序。
  • keep PERSON amused
    语法结构：keep PERSON amused
    该结构限定了此义项可接的补语、介词或语序。

Collocations

  • amuse the children
    逗孩子们开心
  • keep yourself amused
    让自己有事消遣

Chunks

  • amuse oneself by + -ing
    通过做……来自娱

Examples

  • We amused ourselves by inventing stories.
    我们编故事来自娱自乐。
  • The puzzle kept the passengers amused during the delay.
    延误期间，这个谜题让乘客们有事可做，不觉无聊。

Near-Synonym Contrast

  • amuse oneself vs enjoy oneself
    Amuse oneself identifies an activity used to pass time; enjoy oneself states that one had a good time.
    近义辨析：amuse oneself vs enjoy oneself
