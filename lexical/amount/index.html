amount

Core Meaning Overview

Amount names the magnitude of an uncountable quantity and, in amount to, expresses a numerical total or practical equivalence.

数量；总计；等同于

Semantic Map

NOUN

Lexical Element Subcards

amount.N1 · NOUN · quantity of uncountable matter or abstract extent

A quantity of something treated as a mass, substance, time, money, work, or abstract degree rather than as separate countable units.

不可数事物或抽象程度的数量

amount.V1 · VERB · add up to a total

To produce a particular total when quantities are added together.

合计达到某个总数

Grammar Patterns

  • X amounts to + money/number/quantity

amount.V2 · VERB · be equivalent in effect or meaning

To be effectively the same as something, especially a judgment or consequence.

在实际意义或效果上等同于

Grammar Patterns

  • X amounts to + noun/-ing
    语法结构：X amounts to + noun/-ing

Chunks

    实际上等于说……
  • not amount to much
    算不了什么；没有多大价值
  • X amounts to Y
    X amounts to Y
    该表达承载可复用的逻辑关系：Although X may not be labelled Y directly, its practical meaning or effect is Y.

Examples

  • Ignoring the warning amounts to accepting the risk.
    无视警告实际上就等于接受这一风险。

Near-Synonym Contrast

  • amount to vs equal
    Amount to often gives an evaluative interpretation; equal more neutrally states identity or numerical equivalence.
