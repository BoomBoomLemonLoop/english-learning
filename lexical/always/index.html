always

Core Meaning Overview

Always marks universal frequency, continuing time, or emphatically repeated behaviour.

总是；永远

Semantic Map

ADVERB

  • always.ADV1
  • always.ADV2
  • always.ADV3

Lexical Element Subcards

always.ADV1 · ADVERB · on every occasion

On every occasion, or throughout the whole relevant period.

每次都；始终

    语法结构：after be/modal/auxiliary: SUBJECT + BE/AUX + always

Examples

  • She always checks the figures twice.
    她每次都会把数字核对两遍。
  • The emergency exit must always remain clear.
    紧急出口必须始终保持畅通。

always.ADV3 · ADVERB · repeatedly, often annoyingly

Used with a progressive form to emphasize repeated behaviour, commonly expressing irritation or criticism.

老是……，常带不满语气

Grammar Patterns

  • BE always VERB-ing
    语法结构：BE always VERB-ing
    该结构限定了此义项可接的补语、介词或语序。

Examples

  • He is always interrupting people.
    他老是打断别人。

Near-Synonym Contrast

  • always + simple vs always + progressive
    The simple form states a regular fact; the progressive often presents repeated behaviour as noticeable or annoying.
