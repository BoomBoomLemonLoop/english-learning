analyse

Core Meaning Overview

Analyse means systematically separate and examine parts, features, or relations in order to understand or explain something.

分析

Semantic Map

VERB

  • analyse.V1

Lexical Element Subcards

analyse.V1 · VERB · examine systematically

To examine something carefully by separating it into relevant parts, features, or factors in order to understand or explain it.

通过拆分要素与关系进行系统分析

Near-Synonym Contrast

  • analyse vs examine
    Examine means inspect carefully; analyse additionally emphasizes breaking the object into components or relations to explain it.
