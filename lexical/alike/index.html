alike

Core Meaning Overview

Alike expresses similarity, either as a predicative adjective or an adverb describing similar manner.

相像的；以相同方式

alike.ADJ1 · ADJECTIVE · similar to each other

Similar in appearance, character, or other relevant respects.

彼此相像的
