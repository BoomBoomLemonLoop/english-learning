alter

Core Meaning Overview

Alter means make or become different while identity usually remains recognizable; tailoring is a specialized application.

改变；改动；改衣服

alter.V1 · VERB · make something different

To change a feature, condition, plan, or effect, usually without replacing the whole thing.

改变某事物的某些特征，但通常不彻底替换它

alter.V2 · VERB · become different

To change in some respect without an explicitly expressed external agent.

自身在某方面发生变化

Grammar Patterns

  • NOUN + alter(s)
    表达“自身在某方面发生变化”的结构：NOUN + alter(s)
    该结构标明这个意义所要求的补语形式、词序或作用范围；使用时不能只按中文意思自由替换介词或不定式形式。

Collocations

  • circumstances alter
    noun+verb
    情况发生变化
  • attitudes alter
    noun+verb
    态度发生变化

Examples

  • Her expression altered when she heard the news.
    她听到这个消息后，表情变了。

Minimal Contrast

  • 最小对比：The drug altered his mood. / His mood altered.
    两句通过最小变化显示及物结构与不及物结构的参与者差异。
